Our Purpose

ONE IRM Installer was created by IRM Naga Church to use technology as a tool for serving churches and ministries. It provides a centralized platform where churches can access, install, and update useful applications designed for worship, administration, ministry activities, communication, and other church needs.

The initiative also seeks to support the growth of IRM Naga Church, the ministry behind the development of ONE IRM. As the platform grows, part of its long-term purpose is to help support IRM Naga Church in its goal of acquiring land and eventually building its own permanent place of worship.

02
◎

Our Mission

To develop simple, reliable, accessible, and useful digital solutions that can help churches and ministries improve their worship services, administration, communication, and ministry activities.

Through ONE IRM Installer, IRM Naga Church aims to share technology beyond its own congregation by creating tools that other churches can also use and benefit from, while using the initiative to support the continued growth of the ministry.

03
◇

Our Vision

To see ONE IRM grow into a trusted digital platform that provides practical and accessible technology for churches and ministries, regardless of denomination or organization.

We envision an initiative that began at IRM Naga Church reaching and serving other churches through technology, while also contributing to IRM Naga's long-term goal of acquiring its own land and building a permanent church—a place where people can worship God, grow in faith, fellowship, and serve the community.

Built for today. Hopeful for tomorrow.

Every application supports a bigger story.

As ONE IRM grows, it can help create opportunities to support church projects and move IRM Naga Church closer to its dream of having a permanent home.
